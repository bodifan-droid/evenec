22 Dashboard Design Principles

Good dashboards answer questions.

Bad dashboards display everything.

Follow these rules.

  • White background.
  • Dark text.
  • Consistent spacing.
  • Four KPI cards.
  • Two main charts.
  • Interactive filters.

Memory Hook.

One screen.

One story.

23 Step 1 — Build KPI Cards

Create these measures if they don’t already exist.

Total Revenue =
SUM(Orders[amount])

Total Orders =
COUNTROWS(Orders)

Average Order =
AVERAGE(Orders[amount])

Create four Card visuals.

  • Revenue
  • Orders
  • Average Order
  • Top City

Arrange them across the top.

Exactly like executive dashboards.

24 Top City Measure

Create.

Top City =
VAR CityTable =
    SUMMARIZE(
        Customers,
        Customers[city],
        "Revenue",[Total Revenue]
    )

RETURN
MAXX(
    TOPN(1,CityTable,[Revenue]),
    Customers[city]
)

Business Meaning.

The dashboard always displays the strongest city.

36 Business Case

The CEO asks.

Why did revenue fall?

Workflow.

  1. Filter month.
  2. Compare categories.
  3. Drill through.
  4. Explain findings.

Good Answer.

Revenue declined because one category underperformed while London remained stable.

Notice.

Insight.

Not description.

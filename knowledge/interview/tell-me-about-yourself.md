# Tell me about yourself

Category: HR Interview

Difficulty: Easy

---

## Purpose

Introduce yourself clearly in under 60 seconds.

## Recruiter Psychology

The recruiter evaluates:

- structure
- confidence
- communication
- career direction

## Memory Hook

Past → Present → Future

## Model Answer (B2)

> I'm currently working in a dental laboratory in London as a 3D Designer.

> Before this role I ran my own business in Ukraine.

> That experience led me into data analytics, where I learned SQL, Excel, Power BI and Python.

> Now I'm looking for my first Junior Data Analyst role.

## Bogdan Note

Use this exact career transition.

Business → Dental Lab → GoIT → Data Analyst.

## Common Mistake

Don't say:

> "I love numbers."

Use evidence instead.

## Practice

Target:

60 seconds.

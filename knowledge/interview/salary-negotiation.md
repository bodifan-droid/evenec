# Salary Negotiation

Category: HR Interview

Difficulty: Medium

---

## Target Salary

£35,000–45,000

## Best Response

> Based on my research, I'm looking for something in the region of £35,000 to £45,000 depending on the overall package.

## If They Offer Less

> I appreciate the offer.

> I was hoping for something closer to £35,000.

> Is there any flexibility?

## Memory Hook

Research → Range → Flexibility.
